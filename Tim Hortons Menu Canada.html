The Tim Hortons Take Twelve is perfect for sharing hot coffee or tea with a group. It’s ideal for meetings, office breaks, or small gatherings. Each box includes 12 servings, making it an easy and affordable choice for coffee lovers.

You can choose from Original Blend, Dark Roast, Decaf, or Steeped Tea. Each box comes freshly brewed with cups, lids, stir sticks, sugar, and creamers, ready to pour and enjoy.

Customization & Allergens

You can customize your Take Twelve based on the drink type and strength you prefer. Choose from Original Blend, Dark Roast, Decaf, or Tea. Some locations may also offer Specialty Tea or Hot Chocolate. The drink stays warm in an insulated container for up to two hours, so it’s perfect for meetings or road trips.

Since it’s brewed coffee or tea, the drink itself contains no major allergens. However, milk, cream, or sweeteners can add dairy or soy. Each 8 oz serving has around 3 calories, no fat, no cholesterol, 10 mg sodium, and zero sugar.

To serve, set it up as a small coffee station with milk, sugar, and napkins. Keep the lid closed until pouring to maintain the perfect temperature.

Take Twelve pairs perfectly with Tim Hortons donuts, muffins, or bagels. The light flavor of the coffee complements the sweetness of baked goods and the warmth of breakfast items. It’s a simple and satisfying way to complete any group meal or morning spread.
