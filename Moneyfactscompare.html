New £200 switching deal from The Co-operative Bank could help with the cost of Christmas

Those who act quickly could get their hands on an initial £125 payment before the big day.

 

A new current account switching deal from The Co-operative Bank, worth up to £200, could offer consumers a much-needed financial boost in the lead up to and after the expensive festive season.

Eligible new customers* who apply for its Standard Current Account or Everyday Extra Account, as well as existing customers with a qualifying account**, will receive £125 within seven days of completing a full switch from another provider using the Current Account Switch Service (CASS) and meeting all criteria. This includes depositing a minimum of £1,500 into the account, having at least two active direct debits, making five or more debit card transactions and registering for online or mobile banking - all within 30 days of switching.

Those who act quickly might even have the initial lump sum paid into their account in time for Christmas.

Meanwhile, a further £25 is up for grabs in each of the following three months (up to a maximum of £75) when accountholders continue to deposit £1,500 or more, make at least five debit card transactions and maintain a minimum of two active direct debits.

“Christmas can be an expensive time of year, and with so many extra costs to think about, a little additional money can go a long way,” said Kat Robinson, Head of Family and Everyday Banking at The Co-operative Bank. “Whether that extra money helps towards festive plans, goes straight into savings or simply gives someone a bit more breathing room with their everyday spending, we hope it can make a useful difference,” she continued.

 

What is the Current Account Switch Service?

The Current Account Switch Service (CASS) makes it quick and easy to move bank accounts, and has facilitated hundreds of thousands of switches each year since being launched in 2013.

The service enables participating banks and building societies to complete the process of switching a customer's old bank account to their new account within just seven working days (at most), and is free for consumers to use.

Learn more about the Current Account Switch Service.

“Switching isn’t just about the cash incentive”

However, it’s important not to be swayed by cash incentives alone, and to consider all features of a current account carefully to find one that meets your needs and offers the best overall value.

“Switching isn’t just about the cash incentive,” echoed Robinson. For instance, neither The Co-operative Bank’s fee-free Standard Current Account nor its Everyday Extra packaged bank account (costing £12 per month) charge any currency conversion fees when used abroad – which may add to their appeal. What’s more, both accounts unlock exclusive access to the provider’s Regular Saver Issue 1 (offering a competitive 7.00% AER variable) and its prize draw that gives households the chance to win back their bills (up to a maximum of £5,000) until 31 March 2027 (Ts&Cs apply).

Those who opt for the Everyday Extra account will additionally receive mobile phone insurance, worldwide travel insurance, as well as UK & European Breakdown cover.

How does it compare?

This latest offer from The Co-operative Bank joins several other switching deals already on the market. Nationwide BS, for example, also pays eligible customers £175 for switching to its FlexDirect, FlexPlus or FlexAccounts. Alternatively, a slightly higher £200 is available from Lloyds Bank, NatWest and Royal Bank of Scotland when moving to a qualifying account.

Those who swap their existing account for either first direct’s 1st Account or HSBC’s Bank Account, meanwhile, could get an even more substantial £210 or £220 cashback, respectively. Santander and Barclays Bank, however, offer some of the most competitive incentives for switching to a standard, fee-free account with deals worth £240 and £300, respectively.

But, remember to check account features and eligibility criteria carefully before applying for these (or other) offers. If you’re looking to boost your finances this festive season, it’s also important to find out whether any cash perks will reach your bank account in time for Christmas.
