Questions Overview

1. How do you look in the front camera of your phone?
  1. Exactly how I expect.
  2. It depends on the angle.
  3. I feel personally attacked.
2. Your eyes are…
  1. Identical twins
  2. Siblings
  3. Cousins
  4. Two strangers sharing a face
3. What’s your side profile giving?
  1. Greek sculpture
  2. Low-key a cutie
  3. Slightly questionable
  4. A generational fumble
4. How do you feel about being photographed from both sides?
  1. Both sides EAT.
  2. I have a preferred side.
  3. One side is significantly better.
  4. Only one side is allowed to be photographed at all.
5. Now what if someone takes a photo of you from below?
  1. Still gorgeous.
  2. Not my fave angle.
  3. I can feel my aura dropping.
  4. That’s actually a hate crime.
6. How often do people compliment your appearance?
  1. All the time!
  2. Every once in a while.
  3. Only when I’ve made an effort.
  4. Uh, do people really give out compliments like that?
7. If someone says you’re attractive, you…
  1. Get shy but accept it.
  2. Ask if they’re being serious.
  3. Say thank you because obviously!
  4. Assume they’re making fun of me.
8. Have you ever caught someone staring at you?
  1. Yes, I catch people staring all the time.
  2. Hmmm… maybe a few times here and there.
  3. No, and I’m glad it hasn’t happened. That’s weird AF!
9. When you walk past someone, they usually…
  1. Keep walking.
  2. Sneak a glance.
  3. Do a double take.
  4. Make eye contact and smile.
10. Final Q! If you had to do a face reveal, would you survive?
  1. Yes, I’d break the internet in the best way possible.
  2. Nah, the comment section would be MUTED.
  3. Honestly, I’d probably have mixed reviews.

How to Stop Being Chopped

Being chopped is no fun, but luckily, there are lots of ways to un-chop yourself—from developing a simple skincare routine to cleaning up your diet. We spoke with looksmaxxing experts, image consultants, and a licensed aesthetician for tips on how to level up and look your best:

  • Looksmaxxing expert Rorz says fixing your health is the first step to becoming more physically attractive. He recommends eating a clean diet, drinking plenty of water, hitting the gym, and avoiding processed foods. Then, he says you can look into changes like teeth whitening, dyeing your eyebrows and eyelashes, and developing a better fashion sense.[1] X Expert Source Rorz
    Looksmaxxing Expert     Expert Interview
  • Aesthetics and looksmaxxing authority Austin Wayne says starting a skincare routine is one of the top ways to become more attractive. He says it instantly gives your skin a glow and way more moisture, so you’ll age better. He also says a haircut that fits your face shape can work wonders, along with darkening your eyebrows and getting eight to nine hours of sleep each night.[2] X Expert Source Austin Wayne
    Aesthetics and Looksmaxxing Authority     Expert Interview
  • Certified image consultant Sheila A. Anderson says to pay attention to how your clothes fit and invest in a good tailor to save you money in the long run. She adds that leveling up your wardrobe increases your self-belief and shows the outside world that you are serious about upping your game. She adds, “Your style affects your mood, your mindset, and, ultimately, your money. Your visual identity influences how others perceive you and interact with you.”[3] X Expert Source Sheila A. Anderson
    Certified Image Consultant     Expert Interview
  • Image consultant Elle Monus specifically advises men to maintain personal hygiene, since they often don’t have as much room to play with clothes as women. She says a hydrated, well-shaped beard and an up-to-date haircut can take an otherwise basic look to new heights. To top it off, find a scent that is both light and masculine, and spritz just enough for others to smell you as you get close.[4] X Expert Source Elle Monus
    Image Consultant     Expert Interview
  • Licensed esthetician Melissa Jannes notes that trimming your eyebrows with manicure scissors can go a long way for men. She also says to always wear sunscreen, ensure your nails are clean, care for your cuticles, and brush and floss your teeth. She adds that men tend to be a little oilier than women, so mattifying powder can help reduce shine.[5] X Expert Source Melissa Jannes
    Licensed Esthetician     Expert Interview
