Ontario elementary teachers, education workers vote in favour of strike

News | Ontario | Education
Source Canadian Press
By Canadian Press Staff
Share
Elementary Teachers' Federation of Ontario (ETFO) headquarters is seen in Toronto, on Monday, March 9, 2020. Ontario's public elementary school teachers have voted 95 per cent in favour of a strike.THE CANADIAN PRESS/Cole Burston

The Elementary Teachers’ Federation of Ontario says teachers and education workers have overwhelmingly voted in favour of central strike action. 

The federation which represents both groups says 97 per cent of education workers, and 97 per cent of teachers and occasional teachers have voted to give their union strike mandates.

The union's president says the “strong strike mandates” show the increasing challenges educators face in schools and workplaces across the province and underline “our members’ solidarity and support” for the union’s bargaining team.

David Mustin says the vote sends a strong message to the government and the Council of Ontario Directors of Education that the members of the federation are united and ready to take action if needed to bring meaningful improvements to their working conditions. 

The union says it is looking to reach fair agreements at both negotiating tables to deliver improvements on its bargaining priorities that include smaller class sizes, fair compensation, and strengthening health and safety protections. 

Mustin says the members overwhelmingly supported the strike mandates "because they are frustrated by years of chronic underfunding and deteriorating learning and working conditions." 

Bargaining at both negotiating tables for teachers as well as education workers is set to resume at the end of this month. 

This report by The Canadian Press was first published Nov. 18, 2026.
