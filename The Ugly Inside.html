Saints Need To Finally Catch Fire After The International Break
Wednesday, 18th Nov 2026 09:30

Southampton may still be 7th even after the points deduction, as well as having the 4th best record in the Championship in terms of results without that deduction, but they have yet to really catch fire as they did last season, can they get that back into their game.

Saints have been functional in the first third of the season, they have ground out results, but they have also dropped some silly points against teams who are in the bottom half of the table, defeats against Wrexham in 15th, Watford in 20th & the worst of all in the last game before the break the 1-0 reverse to rock bottom Preston, not to mention draws against lowly Lincoln & Bolton were all games were we should really have done better.

It seems that when a side parks the bus, we struggle to break them down, we have lacked the inspiration to break them down with the verve that we showed last season.

Why that is the case is hard to pinpoint, after all from an attacking perspective, it has been mainly the same players used in both the midfield and up front, the only changes being the departure of Ross Stewart and then the arrival of both Lewis Dobbin and Divin Mabuma, so technically we should have more options not less.

It would be easy to point to the issue up front were Cyle Larin has missed the last 4 1/2 games after going off injured just before half time against Bolton, and the fact that since then and indeed before when we lacked the double threat of Larin & Stewart to rotate and offer differing styles.

But it runs deeper than that several players have not yet reached the peaks of last season and perhaps just as important the consistency we had in the second half of last season.

On paper it doesn't look too bad, only Middlesbrough, West Ham & bizarrely Norwich have scored more and only Birmingham have conceded less, but rarely have we been clinical as we were last season.

Then there has been the injury & suspension woes, Caspar Jander having barely played & now Keven Schlotterbeck plus the 3 match suspension for Flynn Downes

So from this perspective it is hard to be critical, but the fact still remains that there is that feeling that we are capable of doing so much better and something is not quite right.

Several players who played big parts last season, seem to have lost favour with Tonda Eckert, Tom Fellows, Welington & Nathan Wood struggling for game time of late.

We seem to rely on a core of the same 11 starting players, the goalkeeper and the back four rarely changing, the midfield trio and wide men the same and up to his injury Cyle Larin started all but one game.

Tonda seems to have lost a bit of faith with his back up players to change things when they aren't going well and this could be the core of the problem.

So when we return from the international break we need to find our fire again, get back to winning ways and more importantly our consistency.

There is no obvious reason why we shouldn't, after all in essence the squad is just as strong as last year apart from the lack of a player to take over the role that Ross Stewart played last term.

We need to get through the next 6 weeks and then perhaps we can find that second striker to share the work with and offer those options we lack up front this year.

But in that time we need to grind out those results, put Derby to the sword on Saturday, Sheffield United on Tuesday and then the big one on Friday when Middlesbrough arrive for a game that really seems to matter to them, they are finding it hard to accept that their failure to gain promotion lays with their own inadequacies and not the fact a young intern took some photos on a field.

It's now taking each game as it comes and that means beating Derby County, do that with panache and the confidence will start to flow back.
