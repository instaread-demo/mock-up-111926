How to Get Six Star Wanted Level in GTA 6 Quickly (Military Base Trick)

  • Visit the Mile Zero Air Station around the Leonida Keys and steal a Rhino tank or a P-996 Lazer fighter jet in GTA 6.
  • Now, destroy the military vehicles and incoming forces to increase your wanted level to six stars.
  • A six-star wanted level in GTA 6 triggers an unstoppable statewide police hunt that cannot be escaped normally.

While causing trouble around Vice City can quickly attract the police, reaching six stars requires a lot more destruction. So, if you are wondering how to get six-star wanted level fast in GTA 6, we have an effective method to share that requires breaking into the military base. So, follow the guide below to reach the maximum stars and heat in GTA 6 without wasting time.

Where to Find the Military Base in GTA 6

Fortunately, the Mile Zero Air Station is located close to the early-game locations where Jason’s main missions in GTA 6 revolve around the Leonida Keys.

You can find this military base on Goose Key in Mariana County, located in the southwestern corner of the GTA 6 map. The base houses military tanks, APCs, combat jets, helicopters, and other restricted vehicles, so stealing them can quickly raise your wanted level in GTA 6.

How to Reach 6 Star Wanted Level Fast in GTA 6

Simply entering the base is not enough to grant you a six-star wanted level. You will need to work for that and might even need to take some hits.

So, here is the step-by-step method to reach six stars in GTA 6 quickly:

Enter the Military Base in GTA 6

The easiest way to enter the base is to take one of your fastest GTA 6 cars, head towards the southwestern entrance, and perform a stunt jump to cross the barbed wire. After landing inside, make your way toward the southern section, where the military tanks are stationed.

Steal the Hauners Rhino tank and prepare for the real fun. Taking it will immediately trigger a three-star wanted level and give you a head start.

Cause Maximum Destruction to Reach Six Stars

Now that you’ve made it halfway through, you need to keep escalating the situation by destroying the incoming military vehicles and attacking the incoming soldiers.

Take advantage of the tank’s armor to ram into other smaller vehicles, and also fire explosives to blast them off. This is where the real fun starts, as you keep shooting and steering clear of being trapped by the military vehicles.

Steal a Military Jet

Since the tank is the closest vehicle with the maximum armor and attacking capabilities near your entry to the base, it is recommended to steal it first. You can then make your way to the fighter jets in GTA 6; they’re stationed inside the military base.

Stealing the P-996 Lazer fighter jet can instantly raise the wanted level to five stars at once.

Moreover, you gain access to homing missiles and a much faster way to escape the situation. This way, you can attack other airborne military units and easily reach the 6-star wanted level in GTA 6.

What Happens When You Get Six Stars in GTA 6?

Once you reach six stars in GTA 6, a prompt pops up on the screen saying, ‘You now have a 6-star wanted level. Escape is Impossible, and the cops won’t give up until you’re hunted down.’

Naturally, unlike the lower wanted levels, you cannot simply break line of sight, change vehicles, or hide in an alley to escape the police chase in GTA 6. Moreover, all your actions in how you’re raising your wanted levels by destroying lives are going to affect your criminal profile in GTA 6.

And if it gets shattered, there is no point of return from the state. So, try to keep your kills within limits and try not to reach the Psychotic behavior a lot during your run.
