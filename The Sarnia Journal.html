Charles Stanley Smith

Peacefully, surrounded by loving family, Charles Stanley Smith passed away at the age of 69 at Bluewater Health on Friday, November 13, 2026.

Charley will be lovingly remembered by his spouse of 45 years, Janette St Jean; his children, Charles, Jason (Holly), Nicholas, Mandy (Kevin) and Randy (Lacey); and his cherished grandchildren, Landon, John, Jaylah and Jayda. He is also remembered by his loving mother, Helen; his brothers, John and Dave (Cathy); his sisters, Pat (Ken), Debbie (Cleave) and Bonnie (Maurice); and his many nieces and nephews. He was predeceased by his father, John (Jack) Smith, and his sister, Jane Smith.

Charley loved working on cars, listening to classic rock and being with his family. He always had a beer in one hand and a smoke in the other. He loved to show off his Corvette and his cherished Great Danes.

The family would like to extend a heartfelt thank you to the staff at Bluewater Health for the care, kindness and compassion shown to Charley.

Charley will be deeply missed, forever loved and never forgotten.

No funeral service will be held. Cremation has already taken place through Lakeview Cemetery Burial and Cremation Services. A celebration of life will be held at a later date.
