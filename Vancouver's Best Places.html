Vancouver Christmas Concerts

    Lots of festive holiday Christmas concerts, choirs and orchestras take place in December each year in Metro Vancouver. Below are some of the best ones.

    Concerts and shows typically happening in mid-December include ones with the Vancouver Welsh Men’s Choir and Chor Leoni. Goh Ballet’s The Nutcracker takes place at the Queen Elizabeth Theatre as well. For an idea of what else to expect, continue reading to learn about the various concerts Metro Vancouver has to offer.

    Note: In addition to major concerts, there is also live music at various holiday attractions including at Merry & Bright, Noel Holiday Light Festival, Vancouver Christmas Market, Shipyards Christmas Market, Dundarave Festival of Lights, Glow Christmas, Wesbrook Holiday Market and others. including Langley Christmas Village which is new for 2026. This article, however, focuses on dedicated concerts during the Christmas season.

    To learn about other winter holiday events this time of year, see our articles about Top Christmas Things to Do and Christmas Holiday Markets.

     

     

    The following choirs and other musical groups perform during the 2026 festive season (or at least performed last year):

    Early Music Vancouver | Goh Ballet Nutcracker | United Voices Choir | Chor Leoni | Vancouver Christmas Market | Vancouver Welsh Men’s Choir | ABRA Cadabra | Songs, Strings & Steps Concerts | Vancouver Oratorio Choir | CPKC Holiday Train | Singing Christmas Tree | Vancouver Symphony | Vancouver Bach Choir

     

     

    Holiday Concerts in Vancouver in November and December of 2026

    Some of the region’s favourite holiday performances include concerts by the Vancouver Welsh Men’s Choir, United Voices Choir, Vancouver Symphony Orchestra, Chor Leoni, Vancouver Bach Choir, and Pacific Baroque Orchestra with Early Music Vancouver. There is also the Goh Ballet Nutcracker which is a ballet, but with an orchestra accompaniment.

     

     

    Christmas Concerts in Vancouver

    Choirs in the section below perform (or will likely perform) over the holiday season in 2026.

    Note: Schedules are subject to change so check concert websites for the most up-to-date information. Also, there are numerous Christmas concerts at schools and churches throughout Metro Vancouver. Keep a lookout for events close to your neighbourhood too!

     

    Early Music Vancouver Concerts

    Early Music Vancouver hosts shows throughout most of the year, including over the Christmas season. In 2026 the performing arts organization presents two concerts in December at the Chan Centre, located at 6265 Crescent Road.

    The 2026 schedule is below (with exact details subject to change).

    • Friday, December 4th, 2026 – A French Baroque Christmas with Ensemble Correspondances at 7:30 pm.
    • Sunday, December 20th, 2026 – Festive Cantatas: Gloria with Vivaldi & Bach at 3:00 pm.

    To learn more about these and other shows by the same organization, see our article about Early Music Vancouver Concerts or click earlymusic.bc.ca.

     

    Goh Ballet’s Nutcracker

    Goh Ballet hosts a special production of The Nutcracker during the Christmas season each year. The performances feature talented dancers and live music by the Vancouver Opera Orchestra. The shows are held at Queen Elizabeth Theatre at 630 Hamilton Street in Vancouver.

    In 2026 the production runs daily from Thursday, December 17th to Sunday, December 20th. There are performances at 7:00 pm on the Thursday, Friday and Saturday, and ones at 2:00 pm on the Saturday and Sunday.

    For more information visit the GohNutcracker.com website or see our article about Goh Ballet.

     

    United Voices Choir Concert

    The United Voices Choir hosts a number of concerts throughout the year, including one with a Christmas theme. In 2026 their holiday concert is Handel’s Messiah Sing-Along. The show takes place on December 19th at the Kerrisdale Presbyterian Church, located at 2733 West 41st Avenue in Vancouver.

    For full details visit voiceschoir.ca or see our United Voices Choir article.

     

     

    Chor Leoni Concerts

    The Chor Leoni Christmas choir concerts take place at St. Andrew’s Wesley United Church at 1022 Nelson Street in Vancouver. The 2026 Christmas with Chor Leoni concerts happen on December 17th, 18th and 19th.

    Click Chor Leoni Choir for more information or visit chorleoni.org.

     

     

    Vancouver Christmas Market Concerts

    The Vancouver Christmas Market offers live entertainment every day of the season. Last year’s market featured two or three bands or musicians playing for a couple of hours per set each day. The exact details for 2026 are to confirmed, but there will be some sort of music happening at the market.

    To see who is performing when, click Vancouver Christmas Market Entertainment Schedule.

     

    Vancouver Welsh Men’s Choir Concerts

    In 2026 the Vancouver Welsh Men’s Choir holds holiday concerts at Vancouver’s Christ Church Cathedral on December 5th and North Vancouver’s Highlands Church on December 11th. Shows also happen at the Massey Theatre in New Westminster on December 6th, St. Dunstan’s Church in Aldergrove on December 12th, and Shaughnessy United Church in Vancouver on December 19th.

    Click Vancouver Welsh Men’s Choir for more information about the group or visit the official vancouverchoir.ca website.

     

    An ABBA Christmas with ABRA Cadabra

    Moon Coin Productions puts on high quality tribute concerts across the Lower Mainland. In 2026 the company presents An ABBA Christmas with ABRA Cadabra. The shows feature dancing and live performances of hit songs by ABBA. Christmas-themed songs are also included.

    The concerts happen on December 1st at North Vancouver’s Centennial Theatre, December 4th at the Bell Performing Arts Centre in Surrey, December 5th at New Westminster’s Massey Theatre, and December 17th at Surrey’s Chandos Pattison Auditorium.

    For details about these shows and similar events, see our article about Vancouver Tribute Band Concerts.

     

     

    Songs, Strings & Steps Christmas Concerts

    Calvin Dyck is the director of the Abbotsford Youth Orchestra. He is also the person behind Songs, Strings & Steps and the Symphony in the Park event in the summer.

    Songs, Strings & Steps’ annual Christmas concerts happen on December 11th and 12th in 2026. The events take place at Central Heights Church at 1661 McCallum Road in Abbotsford.

    For the latest details, visit the calvindyck.com website.

     

     

    Vancouver Oratorio Choir Concerts

    The Vancouver Oratorio Choir is a group of close to 90 Christian vocalists and musicians. In 2026 the group performs a Christmas concert on November 29th at the Evangelical Chinese Bible Church, located at 5110 Marine Drive in Burnaby. The concert is titled “From Hope to Grace” and features music by Mozart, Bach and others.

    Performers typically sing in both English and Mandarin. Admission is free but donations are appreciated.

    See the Vancouver Oratorio Choir website for more information.

     

    CPKC Holiday Train Concerts

    The CPKC Holiday Train is a Christmas tradition. It’s a train that travels across Canada and the United States, making stops at various Lower Mainland cities along the way. At each stop there’s a performance by a band. Everyone who attends is encouraged to bring food or cash donations to help support local food banks.

    Last year the Bare Naked Ladies performed at each stop in the province, which included Agassiz, Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows on December 19th, plus Port Moody and Port Coquitlam on December 20th.

    The featured band and train schedule details for 2026 are to be confirmed.

     

    The Singing Christmas Tree Concerts

    The Singing Christmas Tree is an annual event that’s been taking place for over 50 years. It features around 10 concerts that include choir, vocal ensemble, orchestra, dancing and acrobatic performances at Broadway Church at 2700 East Broadway in Vancouver. Shows in 2025 ran on December 5th, 6th, 12th and 13th. 2026 dates are to be confirmed.

    Admission to the concerts is usually FREE (or by donation) and tickets are available online. The event sells out though, and tickets are usually oversold, so even if you have a ticket be sure to arrive at least an hour early. Also, seating is not reserved but on a first-come first-served basis.

    For more details, see our article about the Singing Christmas Tree.

     

     

    Vancouver Symphony Concerts

    The Vancouver Symphony Orchestra performs its annual Traditional Christmas series of Christmas concerts at various venues throughout the Lower Mainland. The 2026 schedule is listed below.

    (Note: Concert dates and other details are subject to change.)

    • Wednesday, December 9th, 2026 – at 7:00 pm at South Delta Baptist Church in Delta.
    • Friday, December 11th, 2026 – at 4:00 pm and 7:30 pm at the Centennial Theatre in North Vancouver.
    • Saturday, December 12th, 2026 – at 4:00 pm and 7:30 pm at the Bell Performing Arts Centre in Surrey.
    • Sunday, December 13th, 2026 – at 4:00 pm and 7:30 pm at the Orpheum Theatre in Vancouver.

    In addition to the above Traditional Christmas concerts, there are also the following concerts which have a Christmas theme:

    • Thursday, December 17th, and Friday, December 18th, 2026 – Home Alone in Concert at the Orpheum Theatre at 7:00 pm.
    • Saturday, December 19th, and Sunday, December 20th, 2026 – The Muppet Christmas Carol in Concert at the Orpheum Theatre at 7:00 pm on the first day and 2:00 pm on the second day.

    For more details see our article about the Vancouver Symphony Orchestra or visit the vancouversymphony.ca website.

     

     

    Vancouver Bach Choir Concerts

    In 2026 the Vancouver Bach Choir performs at the following places and times:

    (Note: Concert date and details are subject to change.)

    • Sunday, December 6th, 2026 – Christmas with the Bach Choir at 2:00 pm at the Orpheum in Vancouver. The concert typically features hundreds of singers.
    • Saturday, December 12th, 2026 – Handel’s Messiah at 7:30 pm at the Orpheum Theatre.
