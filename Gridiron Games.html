How NFL Implied Team Totals Work:

An implied team total is the number of points a team is projected to score based on the game's point spread and over/under total. It is calculated as (game total - team spread) / 2.

For example, a team favored by 3.5 points in a game with a 49-point total has an implied total of 26.25 points. Its opponent has an implied total of 22.75 points. Together, the two teams' implied totals equal the 49-point game total.
